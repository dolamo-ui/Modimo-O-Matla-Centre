About us

Started by five Methodist Church members working in health in Temba.

What began as care for people who still needed help after leaving hospital has grown into a set of programmes the community relies on.

  1. 2009

    Care after discharge

    The organisation began operating after recognising the need for community-based care and support for people who still required assistance after hospital discharge.

  2. 2014

    HIV Testing Services

    An HIV Testing Services programme was established.

  3. 2021

    Orphaned and vulnerable children

    Support for orphaned and vulnerable children was expanded.

Our programmes

Access to care, continuity of care, and support for vulnerable people.

01HIV testing and prevention

Health education, counselling and testing, condom distribution, IEC materials, community mobilisation, stigma and discrimination support, referrals, TB and STI screening and VMMC-related services.

02Adherence clubs

Follow-up and tracing support for patients who miss appointments, medication collection or viral-load collection, together with health education, screening and CCMDD support.

03Orphaned and vulnerable children

Social, health, educational, psychological and spiritual support, including assistance such as food parcels, school uniforms and stationery.

04Community outreach

Community mobilisation, health education, referrals and outreach activities across Hammanskraal and adjacent provinces.

05Adolescent girls and young women

Mentorship for adolescent girls and young women aged 14–24 as part of the organisation's community support programmes.

06GBV support

Support for victims of gender-based violence through in-house skills development and referrals to appropriate services.

Vision and mission

To deliver healthcare services that enhance the physical, emotional and overall wellbeing of communities in South Africa.

Our mission

To transform community lifestyles by empowering individuals with informed choices about services, HIV prevention methods and referrals to appropriate institutions.

  • Promote access to care
  • Provide HIV and chronic-illness services
  • Support people living with HIV
  • Address stigma and discrimination
  • Educate young people about HIV, STIs and TB
  • Support vulnerable people
